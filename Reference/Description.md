# City Layout

The building are multitiered structures where each tier is rectangular block which decreases in width like a ziggurat or step pyramid. All levels have smooth dark green walls and vertical golden frames on each corner that extend upward and slightly curves outward on top. The base level includes a rounded trapezoidal entrance. Each level is the exact same height. The overall structure should be tall in height and short in width. Buildings often include lower underground levels which are flipped version of the surface part so that the enitre stucture appears like a diamond.

Generally, different buildings use colors to show what it is for although not always. General buildings like houses are often green and gold frames. Communal restaurants are brown and beige frames. Transportation areas like stations are often White and grey. Taller versions of the buildings act like skyscrapers ( Although they aren't very visually different from general buildings and are like a stretched up version ) and are often where most facilities are located.

The paths have rounded corners and are made out of polished diorite with an andesite border. The lamp posts are simple smooth vertical trapezoidal diorite pillars with a fire on top. The layout of the city follows a spread out grid system that flows with the geography and incorporates the environment into it.

## Transportation

Transportation includes canoes which follows similar design as architecture. Generally furniature is minimalistic with rounded corners while following the overall style of the architecture.

Also introducing a new form of transportation which would allow fast travel between different cities ( Same achitecture and similar environment styles although different layout ). This is a spaceship. The building is a multitiered celestial structure where each tier is rectangular block which decreases in width like a ziggurat or step pyramid except is mirrored symmetrically so that it appears like a diamond. All levels have smooth dark green walls and vertical golden frames on each corner that extends and slightly curves outward. The central base level includes rounded diamond entrances on each wall. The other levels include a long horizontal completely rounded windows on each side. Each level is the exact same height. The overall structure should be very tall in height while short in width.

## Culture

The writing system used is written from bottom to top vertically in tall stacks. The appearance of the script flows with smooth straight lines that curve while not intersecting. The appearance of a block is overall taller than width and sometimes takes entire block space. Other times appearing full for the left half with only top half filled on the right side.

Design-wise a common motif like in UI is asymmetrical rounded corners ( e.g., 32px 16px 32px 16px ).

Food can be Fok Iimasai ( Baozi made with lichen bread and stuffed with duck ) and Tlatiiwa ( Drink made from vinegar, milk, mint and sparkling water ), and variations. 

Clothing consists of a long upper shirt that extends in the back like a robe although not all the way down stopping before ankles. Like the architecture, clothing has a main color and an accent. Often it includes symmetrical designs of shapes like diamonds or four pointed stars. The outer shirt often has cutout shapes on the front complementing the design of the inner shirt. The top part of the outer shirt is usually closed or buttoned while going down becomes more opened. The sleeves are cut like leaves or flowers.

## Environment

The environment is a cool and misty birch forest with lichens and ferns.
